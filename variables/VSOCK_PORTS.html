    One vsock port per thing the guest talks to, fixed so the guest image can hardcode them.

    Every entry of ADAPTER_SLOTS needs one. A slot with no port binds nothing on the host and the guest dials a port nobody answers, which is a binding that validates, generates and then fails at the first call -- and it fails quietly, because the tenant is the only one who sees it. A spec holds this list against the slot list rather than trusting them to be edited together.
